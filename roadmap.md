### 4. Juured cube (4D)

A unit tesseract. Fourth axis: **Juured**, `0` pole eestlane → `1` on eestlane.

Options, none of them honest:

1. Rotate the 4-cube in the Juured–Välimus plane and project to 3D. People drift as you twist W.
2. Two cubes: the one you have, and its shadow in Juured-space.
3. A real 4D WebGL renderer. Does not exist. Will not exist.

Data is already there (`juured` in `src/people.js`). The missing piece is a fourth orthogonal direction and a graphics card that believes in it.

Do this last, or never. The panel already tells the truth.
